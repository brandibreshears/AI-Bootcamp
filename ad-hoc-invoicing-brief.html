Ad-Hoc Member Invoicing

Driver: Brandi Breshears Approver: Colleen Spicher Last updated: Jun 18, 2026 Status: In Design

Problem

Two security risks, not one: (1) Billing team uses Clover (no active contract) for ad-hoc invoices. (2) Surgery invoices today send a raw Stripe payment link — no auth gate — so any person with the link can pay it. CSS has accidentally sent the wrong link to the wrong member, causing payment mis-attribution, broken accumulators, and manual remediation across Primetime, Stripe, and Netsuite.
  • Manual invoice creation is slow, error-prone, and spread across multiple systems.
  • No formal approval workflow or audit trail exists today.
  • Scope spans Care at Home, Cancer COE, Chemo/Radiation, Ortho consults, Telehealth mis-charges, and misc. M&I reimbursements — none handled inside Primetime.
  • Accumulator data exists in Orbit but is not surfaced during invoice creation, causing inaccurate cost-share amounts.
  • ~⅓ of Surgery invoicing routes through Clover today specifically because members want to split payment across two cards — no native path exists.
  • Non-app registered members with a UUID have no secure self-service payment path.

Solution in One Paragraph

Admin side (Primetime): Build a unified ad-hoc invoicing flow inside Primetime — member search → invoice type → details → accum check → approval queue → issue. Replace Clover. Extend the state machine with Draft / Pending Approval / Correction Required states.

Member side (Member App + web): Replace raw Stripe links with a single static URL (transcarent.com/profile/me/invoice). Identity-gate payment — member's verified UUID determines what invoice they see, not which link they followed. Works for app-registered and non-app members with a UUID alike.

Two Invoice Types

Member Cost Share

Collect money owed by member for a care event. Requires accumulator check against Orbit to determine actual dollar amount.

  • Encounter Type (Care@Home, COE, Chemo, Radiation, Ortho, Telehealth, Surgery, Other)
  • Maximum Member Cost → adjusted after accum check
  • Copay / Coinsurance / Differential DED (manual if no config)
  • Service Date, Due Date, Case Rate, Case Number (situational)
  • EOB fields when Surgery EOB policy flag is On

Member Recoupment

Claw back an overpayment made to member (e.g., cancelled surgery M&I). No accumulator check.

  • Encounter Type (same dropdown)
  • Member Repayment Amount (fixed, no calculation)
  • Service Date, Due Date, Case Number (situational)

Member-Facing Payment Experience (New)

Architecture shift: Today Surgery uses a raw Stripe-hosted link (no auth). New model is a single static URL — member's identity resolves the invoice. No member-specific link is ever generated or emailed.

Single Static URL

transcarent.com/profile/me/invoice — same for every member, every invoice type. Billing team can share it freely without risk of routing to the wrong person. Invoice is retrieved based on who you are, not what link you followed.

Scope: Members with a Transcarent UUID only. Members without a UUID must be invoiced manually via the Stripe portal (out of scope).

Two Authentication Paths

App-Registered Member

  • Lands at URL, logs in with existing TC credentials
  • UUID resolves → outstanding invoices retrieved
  • Sees invoice label (e.g. "Ortho Consult Member Cost Share"), amount, and PDF viewable pre-payment
  • Post-payment: receipt PDF downloadable; history in app
  • Can also reach via in-app Payments section (existing path continues)

Non-App Member (UUID required)

  • Lands at URL, completes lightweight identity verification (1FA or 2FA — TBD with Identity team)
  • UUID resolves → outstanding invoices retrieved
  • Sees generic label ("Invoice 1"), amount, and card entry only — no PHI/PII displayed
  • No app download required before or during payment
  • Post-payment: prompt to complete app registration; receipt available after

Multiple Invoice Handling

  • 1 invoice: Land directly on payment screen.
  • 2 invoices: Both surfaced — layout TBD (sequential vs. simultaneous — design decision open). Each has its own card entry form and Pay button; card details must be re-entered per invoice (Stripe constraint: can't apply one entry to multiple payment intents).
  • 3+ invoices (edge case): Show 2 oldest-due-date invoices. Display notice to contact support. Billing team alerted for manual handling.

Split Card Payments

Not self-service. Member must contact CSS/Member Support. Billing team cancels original invoice and creates two new ad-hoc invoices in the requested amounts, issued through the standard flow.

Open Questions & Risks

Non-app member invoicing decision is unresolved — MFA gate, no-PHI PDF, or direct Stripe link. This impacts PDF templates, Braze campaigns, and email copy. Must decide before Member-facing work begins.
Blocker
Netsuite scripts: do they work without a Salesforce case number? Ad-hoc invoices for Care@Home/Ortho/etc. have no SF ID. Needs Netsuite team confirmation before generate_invoice transition can be completed.
Decision
TC4TC permissions on approval queue — should surgery invoice screens also be gated? (Currently they may not be.) Align with existing surgery permissions before building approval UI.
Decision
Should the submitter be able to self-reject / retract a pending-approval invoice? Workflow doc says no same-person approval, but self-retract is a usability gap.
TBD
What identifier (if not Case Number) ties ad-hoc invoices to Netsuite records? "Invoice Reference Number" is proposed — needs naming alignment with finance.
TBD
Split-CC (split credit card across two cards for surgery + ad-hoc) explicitly deferred to post-MVP. Confirm Stripe supports it for future planning.
TBD
Prior-year billing: must handle members who terminated coverage. Invoice for 2025 service sent in Jan 2026 needs coverage-period validation, not just current eligibility.
Scoped out
New PDF templates for ad-hoc (non-surgery) invoice types — separate ticket. MVP uses recycled Surgery template for app-registered users only.

Member-Facing Open Questions

Blocker
1FA vs 2FA for non-app auth: What factors are used? Skip Big 4 and go straight to a challenge question (e.g. DOB, SSN last 4)? Is 1FA sufficient to initiate payment, or is 2FA required before any payment can process? Owner: Brandi / Identity / Legal.
Decision
Multi-invoice layout: Sequential (Invoice 1 loads first; Invoice 2 loads after Invoice 1 is paid) or simultaneous (both on the same page)? Design team to decide before build. Either approach requires separate card entry per invoice.
Decision
Identity team's mechanism for UUID-bound guest auth and UUID pre-association at registration. Needed before non-app member flow can be scoped for eng.
TBD
Terminated member with outstanding prior-year invoice — how does the invoice screen handle a member whose coverage has ended but a 2025 invoice is still outstanding?
TBD
Post-payment registration prompt copy: what 2–3 specific benefits should be highlighted? (Receipt PDF, payment history, care navigation?) Owner: Product / Marketing.
