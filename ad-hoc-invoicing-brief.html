Ad-Hoc Member Invoicing

Driver: Brandi Breshears Approver: Colleen Spicher Last updated: Jun 18, 2026 Status: In Design

Problem

Security risk: Billing team currently uses Clover, a legacy tool with no active contract, to generate ad-hoc member invoices. This creates security exposure and must be decommissioned.
  • Manual invoice creation is slow, error-prone, and spread across multiple systems.
  • No formal approval workflow or audit trail exists today.
  • Scope spans Care at Home, Cancer COE, Chemo/Radiation, Ortho consults, Telehealth mis-charges, and misc. M&I reimbursements — none handled inside Primetime.
  • Accumulator data exists in Orbit but is not surfaced during invoice creation, causing inaccurate cost-share amounts.

Solution in One Paragraph

Build a unified ad-hoc invoicing flow inside Primetime that lets the billing team search for a member, choose an invoice type (Cost Share or Recoupment), enter invoice details, run an accumulator check, submit for approval, and trigger a Stripe payment link — all without leaving the tool. Replace Clover entirely. Extend the existing billable-event state machine with new Draft / Pending Approval / Correction Required states. Route payments through the correct Stripe account per encounter type and push to Netsuite where applicable.

Two Invoice Types

Member Cost Share

Collect money owed by member for a care event. Requires accumulator check against Orbit to determine actual dollar amount.

  • Encounter Type (Care@Home, COE, Chemo, Radiation, Ortho, Telehealth, Surgery, Other)
  • Maximum Member Cost → adjusted after accum check
  • Copay / Coinsurance / Differential DED (manual if no config)
  • Service Date, Due Date, Case Rate, Case Number (situational)
  • EOB fields when Surgery EOB policy flag is On

Member Recoupment

Claw back an overpayment made to member (e.g., cancelled surgery M&I). No accumulator check.

  • Encounter Type (same dropdown)
  • Member Repayment Amount (fixed, no calculation)
  • Service Date, Due Date, Case Number (situational)

Workflow (10 Steps)

1. Member Search
2. Invoice Type + Encounter
3. Invoice Details
4. Accum Check (Cost Share only)
5. Review & Submit
6. Approval Queue
7. Approve / Reject
8. Invoice Sent to Member
9. Stripe Payment
10. Receipt + Payment History

Open Questions & Risks

Non-app member invoicing decision is unresolved — MFA gate, no-PHI PDF, or direct Stripe link. This impacts PDF templates, Braze campaigns, and email copy. Must decide before Member-facing work begins.
Blocker
Netsuite scripts: do they work without a Salesforce case number? Ad-hoc invoices for Care@Home/Ortho/etc. have no SF ID. Needs Netsuite team confirmation before generate_invoice transition can be completed.
Decision
TC4TC permissions on approval queue — should surgery invoice screens also be gated? (Currently they may not be.) Align with existing surgery permissions before building approval UI.
Decision
Should the submitter be able to self-reject / retract a pending-approval invoice? Workflow doc says no same-person approval, but self-retract is a usability gap.
TBD
What identifier (if not Case Number) ties ad-hoc invoices to Netsuite records? "Invoice Reference Number" is proposed — needs naming alignment with finance.
TBD
Split-CC (split credit card across two cards for surgery + ad-hoc) explicitly deferred to post-MVP. Confirm Stripe supports it for future planning.
TBD
Prior-year billing: must handle members who terminated coverage. Invoice for 2025 service sent in Jan 2026 needs coverage-period validation, not just current eligibility.
Scoped out
New PDF templates for ad-hoc (non-surgery) invoice types — separate ticket. MVP uses recycled Surgery template for app-registered users only.
